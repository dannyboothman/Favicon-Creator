Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Favicon Creator is a Chrome extension that helps you design and download favicons. This page explains what data the extension handles and how it is used.

Data stored on your device

Design choices (text, colours, fonts, borders, and related options), theme preference, download options, and favicon history are saved in Chrome’s local storage on your device so the extension can remember your settings between sessions. This data stays on your computer and is not uploaded to our servers. You can clear all local storage from the Settings page at any time.

Active tab preview

When you open the extension, it may temporarily update the favicon on your current browser tab so you can preview your design. That access is limited to the active tab while the extension popup is open, and the original favicon is restored when you close it. Page content is not collected or transmitted.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts — If you choose a Google Font other than the bundled Montserrat font for preview, the extension loads that font from Google’s servers. Google may receive the font family name and standard network request information. See Google’s privacy policy for details.
  • PayPal — The optional “Buy Me a Coffee” donate form on Settings is provided by PayPal. If you use it, PayPal’s terms and privacy policy apply to that transaction and any related requests (including PayPal’s donate button assets).

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show ads, or send your favicon designs, history, or browsing activity to our own servers. The extension has no analytics or tracking of its own.
